WHAT ARE GALACTIC ENTITIES?

Galactic Entities is a collection of 10,000 unique cosmic being NFTs on the Ethereum blockchain. Each NFT is algorithmically generated from hundreds of astronomical assets and gives you access to our growing Galactic Universe.

HOW MUCH DOES IT COST TO MINT?

The mint price is 1.5 ETH and the reveal will happen 24 hours after mint-out. There are no bonding curves or price increases - the mint price stays the same until the last NFT is claimed.

WHEN IS THE MINT DATE?

The cosmic gates open on August 15th, 2023 at 12:00 PM EST. Set a reminder and make sure your ETH is ready!

HOW DO I PREPARE FOR MINT?

1. Get an Ethereum wallet like MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet
2. Buy ETH and have at least 1.6 ETH in your wallet (for mint + gas fees)
3. Connect your wallet when the countdown hits 0
4. Click MINT and approve the transaction
5. Watch as your Galactic Entity emerges from the cosmos!
